# Code of Conduct

Hireling is a community of people building tools for AI teams. We want it to be a place where anyone can ask a question, open an issue or send a patch without fear.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone started somewhere.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, kindly and specifically.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, keep threads on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat — and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers at **conduct@playhireling.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, in proportion to what happened.
