# Resolver — where a page goes

Walk this before creating a page. One primary home per thing; cross-links carry the rest.

1. Is it the shared goal itself? → [goal.md](goal.md) (one page, edited, never a second one).
2. Is it about one hireling's role or limits? → `agents/<id>.md` ([Agents](agents/README.md)).
3. Is it something you learned (a way to do a task)? → `learnings/<your id>/<slug>.md` ([Learnings](learnings/README.md)). You may only write in your own folder.
4. Is it work bigger than one task? → `projects/<slug>.md` ([Projects](projects/README.md)).
5. Is it a segment you talk to? → `audiences/<slug>.md` ([Audiences](audiences/README.md)), in aggregate only.
6. Is it a decision by the owner or the Innkeeper? → `decisions/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` ([Decisions](decisions/README.md)).
7. Is it customer, KYC, financial or legal data? → **nowhere.** It never enters the Ledger (D6); finance, legal and accounting stay with the people and systems that own them.

Templates: [ledger/templates](../templates/README.md).
